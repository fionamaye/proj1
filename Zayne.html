Zayne

Zayne is a world-renowned cardiac surgeon, known for his groundbreaking research on congenital heart defects. However, beyond his public identity, he is also deeply involved in the study of Evol genes—the mysterious genetic mutations that grant certain individuals supernatural abilities. His knowledge of Evol biology is not just professional but also personal, as he possesses an Evol ability himself, though its true nature is revealed gradually through his story.
