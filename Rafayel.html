Rafayel

Rafayel is an eccentric and enigmatic artist, known for his breathtaking paintings that seem almost otherworldly. Unlike Xavier or Zayne, who have more grounded and practical roles in the fight against the Wanderers, Rafayel exists in a realm of dreams, visions, and abstract thought. His connection to the protagonist is deeply spiritual and intertwined with the overarching mystery of the game’s universe.
